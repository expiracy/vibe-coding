## Make Meaningful Distinctions

Distinguish concepts by their domain role rather than arbitrary numbers or filler words. `source` and `destination` explain a relationship that `data1` and `data2` conceal.

If two names cannot be distinguished meaningfully, examine whether they represent the same concept or whether the interface is missing context. Do not create redundant types just to produce different names.

Reference: [Clean Code, Chapter 2: Meaningful Names](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/clean-code-a/9780136083238/)
