## Make the Design Express Its Intent

Choose concepts, names, and operations that explain the domain directly. Avoid clever encodings or pattern names that require their own explanation when a familiar concept would suffice.

Use representative examples and behavioral checks to explain what matters. Preserve the project's vocabulary and do not mistake a readable rewrite for evidence that its behavior is correct.

Reference: [Clean Code, Chapter 12: Emergence](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/clean-code-a/9780136083238/)
