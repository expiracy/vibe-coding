## Follow Team Formatting Rules

Use the project's formatting configuration and established conventions. Consistency lets readers concentrate on meaning rather than differences in personal style.

Keep necessary formatting changes local to the task. Resolve conflicting instructions through the project's documented policy instead of silently replacing its configuration.

Reference: [Clean Code, Chapter 5: Formatting](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/clean-code-a/9780136083238/)
